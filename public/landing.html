What it checks

01

Was it reviewed

For each change that went in, Flaike lists who looked at it, or that nobody did.

02

Was a review required

It checks whether the main branch actually requires a review before a change can be merged.

03

Can the rule be skipped

It notes if an admin can bypass the review, and whether tests or a force push are left open.

What you get

A verdict

One line at the top. How many problems, and what they are. Not a pile of raw data you have to read yourself.

A report

A plain file you can send. Each change, who reviewed it, the date, and the link back to the source.
